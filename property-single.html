Shriji Sharnam

Pushpanjali Baikhunth, Vrindavan, Uttar Pradesh

Shriji Sharnam is Morvi Infratech’s flagship residential project in Pushpanjali Baikhunth, minutes from the sacred rhythms of Vrindavan. The tower blends efficient floor plans, secure access, and devotional proximity for families, NRIs, and seva institutions seeking a trusted second home.

As an end-to-end real estate company, we steward the full journey: transparent booking documentation, construction-quality oversight, customization within sanctioned guidelines, and handover support. Morvi Infratech also delivers construction projects, land transactions, and rentals elsewhere in Braj—this page anchors our signature development.
