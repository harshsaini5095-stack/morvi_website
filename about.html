About Morvi Infratech

Morvi Infratech is an end-to-end real estate company headquartered in Vrindavan. We combine development, contracting, land brokerage, and leasing so families, institutions, and investors can work with a single accountable partner.

Our flagship project, Shriji Sharnam, rises in Pushpanjali Baikhunth with thoughtfully planned residences that honour the rhythms of pilgrimage while meeting modern safety and efficiency standards.

Beyond the tower, we manage construction projects for independent landowners, facilitate transparent land purchases and exits, and operate a rentals desk for devotees and businesses that need dependable housing inventory.

Every mandate begins with listening: are you securing a spiritual second home, monetising inherited acreage, or launching a seva campus? Once the goal is clear, we map title diligence, civic approvals, cash-flow milestones, and handover criteria in plain language.

The Morvi team includes civil supervisors, documentation specialists, and hospitality-minded leasing managers who already navigate Mathura district processes daily. That proximity keeps decisions fast and grounded in ground reality—not distant theory.
