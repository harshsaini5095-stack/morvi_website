Compare packages

Four ways to build with Morvi Infratech. Aashiyana and Haveli are complete homes. Structure Only covers the civil shell. Interior Only covers finishes when the structure is already in place.

Complete home

Aashiyana

Standard package. Structure, fittings, and finishes for a well-built family home.

Complete home

Haveli

Luxury package. Higher-grade materials, fittings, and finishes throughout the home.

Civil shell

Structure Only

Foundation, frame, blockwork, and roof. Finishes are left for a later stage.

Finishes

Interior Only

Kitchen, baths, flooring, paint, doors, and wiring for a home that is already built.
